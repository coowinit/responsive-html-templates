Frequently Asked Questions

Find useful answers to common questions about our products, project requirements, customization, and ongoing support. Explore the information below to plan your next project with greater confidence.

What types of products and solutions do you offer?

We offer a range of products for indoor, outdoor, and architectural applications. Our team can help you compare available systems and identify options that suit your project goals, environment, and design requirements.

Can products be customized for different projects?

Customization options may include dimensions, finishes, colors, and selected product specifications. Share your drawings or requirements with our team so we can review feasibility and confirm the details before an order is placed.

How do I choose the right material for my application?

Start with the intended environment, installation conditions, appearance, maintenance expectations, and budget. Product documentation and technical discussions can help you narrow the options; final suitability should be confirmed against project-specific requirements.

Do you provide technical documents and installation guidance?

Relevant specifications, installation guidance, and product documents can be discussed during the quotation process. Document availability depends on the selected product and project, so please ask for the resources you need.

What information is needed to request a quotation?

Please include the product type, estimated quantity, preferred specifications, delivery destination, and project schedule where available. Clear requirements help our team prepare a more useful response and identify any details that still need confirmation.

How are lead times and delivery arrangements confirmed?

Lead times and shipping arrangements depend on product selection, quantity, customization, and destination. The agreed schedule should be confirmed as part of the order and project communication process.
