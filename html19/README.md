# Featured Blog and Recent Posts

A responsive editorial layout with one highlighted article and three related posts.

## Layout

- Desktop: a large featured article on the left and three compact horizontal article rows on the right.
- Mobile: the featured article appears first, followed by three horizontal related-post rows.
- Uses a wide image placeholder for the featured story and square placeholders for related posts.
- Built with standalone HTML and CSS. No framework or JavaScript dependency.

## Files

- `index.html` — article structure and sample content
- `css/style.css` — responsive layout and typography
- `images/` — four gray SVG image placeholders

## Customization

Replace the SVG files in `images/` with real article images, or update the image paths in `index.html`.
Edit the categories, titles, dates, comment counts, and excerpt directly in the HTML.
The sample article links use `#`; replace them with the corresponding article URLs when integrating.
